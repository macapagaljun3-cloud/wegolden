About

An established group, since 2021

WeGolden is a multi-licensed brokerage group serving traders in more than a dozen languages, with offices across five regions.

Who we are

Who we are

WeGolden began with a single conviction: that gold traders were being underserved on execution and pricing. Five years later we operate under three regulatory regimes, hold client funds across segregated accounts, and serve a global client base from offices in Canada, Uruguay, Cyprus, Dubai and Vietnam. Our product range has grown from gold to more than ninety instruments, but the operating principle has not changed — transparent pricing, fast execution and support that answers.
