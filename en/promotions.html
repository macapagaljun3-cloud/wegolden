Welcome IB Reward: the qualifying test

The programme is evaluated daily against a fixed six-month window that begins on the date your IB registration is approved. All three conditions below must be met at the same time — satisfying two of them does not qualify.

USD 88,000

IB Tree Net Deposit must reach at least USD 88,000 across the clients beneath your code.

Two positive months

Two consecutive months in which Net Deposit is positive, measured within the same six-month window.

20 qualified clients

At least 20 Qualified Clients, counting direct referrals only — not the wider tree.

Who counts as a Qualified Client

A client must satisfy all four criteria below. Meeting three of them is not enough, and the trading test is deliberately strict: only closed positions that moved at least three pips are counted.
