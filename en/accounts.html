Which one is right for you?

ECN

For traders who want raw spreads and are comfortable paying a transparent per-lot commission. Usually the lowest total cost for active, high-volume trading.

Standard

For traders who prefer one simple number. Fixed spreads on FX and gold, no commission, and full bonus eligibility.

Cent

For traders testing a strategy or starting small. Positions are sized in cents, so risk per trade is a fraction of a standard lot. Not eligible for bonuses.

Fixed leverage by asset class

Forex leverage is client-selectable up to 1:1000. Every other asset class carries a fixed maximum that does not change with account equity.

Free swap, applied by default

Every ECN, Standard and Cent account is swap-free from the moment it is opened — in every country we serve.

No request needed

Free swap is switched on automatically. There is no activation request, no form to submit and no waiting period before it applies.

No minimum deposit

There is no deposit threshold to qualify. A newly funded account holds positions overnight on exactly the same terms as a large one.

All three accounts

ECN, Standard and Cent are all swap-free, so overnight financing does not eat into a position you intend to hold.

Already trading with us?

Existing accounts move to the new fee group once all open positions are closed. If you would rather switch immediately, you can open a new account and trade the new Standard or ECN conditions right away — there is no need to wait for existing positions to be closed first.
